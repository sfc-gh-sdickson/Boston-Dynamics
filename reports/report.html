Headline KPIs

Key insights

  • Revenue is growing, but deals are harder to close. Closed-won bookings reached $9.15M (+4.0%) and billings $3.80M (+4.6%). The win rate fell from 85.7% to 70.5%, so more of the deals that closed were lost.
  • Cash collection is strong. Collections of $4.05M (+11.3%) exceeded billings. Receivables still stand at $4.16M, including $0.74M overdue across 16 invoices.
  • Operational load is rising. Incidents rose 17% to 129 and downtime 25% to 2,307 hours. Maintenance spend rose 26% to $1.40M. Of the 129 incidents, 23 were Critical.
  • Support is the top risk. Tickets rose 47% to 301 and SLA attainment fell to 58.4%. CSAT dropped from 3.76 to 3.47. Tickets spiked on Oct 26–27 (24 and 27 per day, against about 11 normally), which may signal an emerging fleet-wide issue.
  • The pipeline looks healthy. $40.6M is open, including $14.7M in Commit. Contracted ARR (active plus renewed) is $99.0M. The first NPS wave scored +33 (n=90).
